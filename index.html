01 — Manifesto

We believe a website should not be read. It should be felt — a living organism of light, matter and motion that responds to every glance, every scroll, every heartbeat.

AETHER fuses real-time 3D, shader-driven visuals and obsessive typography into experiences that behave more like cinema than software. Every pixel is choreographed; every millisecond of motion is intentional.

From morphing liquid metals to particle constellations reacting to your cursor, we build digital objects with physical soul — then optimise them ruthlessly so they fly on any device, anywhere on Earth.
